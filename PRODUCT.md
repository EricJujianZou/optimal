# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People stuck in a real moment — phone in hand — who need a clear call on an everyday choice (tip, cancel plans, reply to a text) and occasionally a heavier life decision (job, rent, relationship). They want speed for small calls and calm depth when the stakes rise, without switching tools.

## Product Purpose

Megamind is a life decision advisor: speak or type a situation and get a clear recommendation, why it wins, and optional spoken counsel. Live facts are gathered when they flip the answer. Clarifying questions appear only when a missing personal fact would change #1. Success is the user leaving clearer and more decisive — not entertained by chat.

## Brand Personality

Calm · Trusted · Steady. A wise friend who commits to a call without hedging or hype. Short sentences. No frantic energy. Stillness is the brand.

## Anti-references

- Generic dark AI chat shells (ChatGPT / Claude-like message threads)
- Wellness soft pastel mindfulness
- Neon cyberpunk / “AGI dashboard” glow
- Corporate SaaS defaults (Inter + purple gradients)

## Design Principles

1. **Verdict first** — The primary object is a Decision Sheet, not a message list. Chat is a fallback, not the metaphor.
2. **Commit, then explain** — Pick a recommendation. Alternatives never visually compete with the call.
3. **Two speeds, one chrome** — Everyday density is fast; heavy density expands why, tradeoffs, and counsel without changing the product language.
4. **Scarce clarify** — Ask only questions that move the recommendation; show why the question matters.
5. **Memory as evidence** — What Megamind knows appears in the argument (and is editable), not as an admin sidebar bolted onto the header.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on dark surfaces. Full `prefers-reduced-motion` support (crossfade / instant instead of motion). Large tap targets on voice controls. Dark-only theme.
